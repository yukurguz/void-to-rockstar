0627	House: genre profile
0628	House was born in Chicago clubs. Its tempo is 120 to 128 beats per minute: set 124.
0629	House harmony is seventh chords from soul and disco. Take them short and choppy, like hits.
0630	The bass plays between kicks, on the ands. Play the line short and springy.
0631	Recipe: kick on every beat, clap on 2 and 4, open hat between kicks, short chords and sidechain from the kick. Learn the beat in the next lesson on the pads. Press Done.

0633	House beat
0634	Kick on every beat, clap on the second and fourth, open hat exactly between the kicks. Hi-hat sixteenths add the motion.
0635	Techno: genre profile
0636	Techno came from Detroit and Berlin: faster and harsher than house. Set the tempo from 128 to 140.
0637	Techno has almost no chords, just a riff of two or three notes. Phrygian mode here: the dark semitone from E to F.
0638	The main hero of techno is the filter: for minutes one riff changes only by the Freq and Res knobs. Turn them as you play it and press Done.
0639	Recipe: heavy kick on every beat, hat between the kicks, reverb rumble under the kick, one hypnotic riff and slow filter movement. Press Done.
0640	Techno is hypnosis: minimum notes, maximum sound. Timbre changes, not melody.
0641	Techno beat
0642	Straight kick, open hat between the hits and dry rim clicks in unexpected places. A tom at the end closes the loop.
0643	Drum and bass: genre profile
0644	Drum and bass grew out of British jungle. Tempo from 170 to 176: the drums fly, while bass and chords move at half the speed. Set 174.
0645	In melodic drum and bass long soft chords float over the fast beat. Hold each one longer.
0646	The bass is low and singing. Harder styles make it from two detuned saws with a half-closed filter: the Reese bass.
0647	Recipe: kick on 1, snare on 2 and 4, between them a second, shifted kick, fast hi-hats. The beat is broken, not straight. Learn it in the next lesson. Press Done.
0648	Drum and bass is drum speed and bass depth. There is a lot of air between them.
0649	Drum and bass beat
0650	Two-step: kick, snare, shifted kick, snare. At tempo 174 this pattern turns into flight.
0651	Dubstep: genre profile
0652	Dubstep came from London. Tempo 140, but the beat is counted at half speed: kick on 1, snare on 3. Set 140.
0653	The mark of the genre is the wobble: a bass whose filter sways to an LFO in rhythm. In Drift pick LFO as the Freq Mod source and set the LFO itself to Sync mode. Try it and press Done.
0654	The bass plays long low notes and the wobble chops them into bits. Play the line, holding each note longer.
0655	Recipe: half-time beat, huge snare with reverb on 3, pauses and wobbling bass. Space matters more than the number of notes. Press Done.
0656	Dubstep is a bass that talks and a silence that hits harder than notes.
0657	Dubstep beat
0658	A half-time beat: the snare lands rarely and hard. All the room between hits goes to bass.
0659	Garage: genre profile
0660	Garage comes from London of the nineties. Tempo from 128 to 136: set 132.
0661	Soul chords played short and scattered, often on organ or electric piano.
0662	The bass is bouncy and springy: it plays around the kick, not with it.
0663	Recipe: kick on 1 and shifted before 3, clap on 2 and 4, hats with swing, chopped vocals. The beat is not straight: it stumbles and so it grooves. Press Done.
0664	Garage is swing and a bouncy bass: dance music that does not march but skips along.
0665	Garage beat
0666	The kick is not where you expect it, and the clap keeps count. Play with a light swing, holding back every second hat.

0684	Lo-fi: genre profile

0693	Phonk grew out of nineties Memphis rap, and drift phonk spread across the world from Russia. Tempo from 130 to 160: set 140.
